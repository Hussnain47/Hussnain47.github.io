Building a financing chatbot from scratch.

Built from scratch and led the team behind a chatbot later used in financing deals worth millions of dollars.

ContextAI product engineering · Vartana
My roleChatbot implementation and team leadership
TechnologyPython / FastAPI / LangGraph / LangSmith

The problem

Financing conversations arrived through SMS, the website, and email. A useful chatbot needed to connect those inputs to the business systems that supported financing workflows.

My contribution

I built the chatbot from scratch and led the team delivering it. I also worked with shareholders on the initiative, connecting the engineering work with the business context.

Connecting the stack

The chatbot used Python and FastAPI, with LangGraph and LangSmith. It connected to GraphQL APIs and a Ruby on Rails server, bringing conversational inputs into the existing application environment.

One product, multiple channels

SMS, website, and email were all input channels. The scope went beyond a standalone chat interface: the product had to connect conversations to the APIs and server behind the financing workflow.

Outcome

The chatbot was later used in financing deals worth millions of dollars, connecting conversational inputs with the existing financing systems.

What I took from the project

This project combined building the chatbot myself, leading the team, connecting existing systems, and working with shareholders on the business side.
