Payments and subscription promotions at Educative.

Integrated Razorpay, worked with Stripe, and built the foundation for recurring and non-recurring subscription discounts.

ContextPayments engineering · Educative
My roleFull-stack payments and promotion development
TechnologyRazorpay / Stripe / Subscriptions / Promotions

What I worked on

I worked on payments and subscriptions at Educative, including the Razorpay integration, Stripe-related workflows, and the foundation for discount promotions on the website.

Adding Razorpay

I integrated Razorpay to improve payment reliability in regions where Stripe had higher failure rates. The work also covered cancellation and chargeback handling, so the integration went beyond the initial payment.

Building the promotion foundation

I built the base for discount promotions across subscriptions. That included recurring discounts and non-recurring discounts, which needed different treatment in the subscription experience.

Working across payments and product

This work connected the customer-facing website with payment providers and subscription behavior. A promotion needed to make sense both when a customer chose it and when the subscription was billed again.

The result

The Razorpay integration added another payment option, and the promotion work established a base for subscription discounts. I also built full-stack payment features and received Educative’s highest performance-review rating, “Greatly Exceeds.”

What I took from it

Payments work made me pay attention to the whole customer journey, including what happens after the first successful charge. Subscription discounts, cancellations, and chargebacks all belong in that picture.
